This bundt cake is as simple as it gets. That's probably why it's one of my favorites. Laced with cardamom (that fantastic lemony spice) it is straightforward, has one of the best crumbs I ever came across and needs no more than a dusting of powdered sugar.

## Ingredients

- 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour (or cake flour)
- 2 ½ teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoons salt
- ¾ cup unsalted butter (at room temperature)
- 1 ¾ cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons ground fresh cardamom (about 30 pods, see Notes, below)
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon lemon zest
- 3 eggs (room temperature)
- ¾ cup whole milk (at room temperature)
- ½ cup sour cream (at room temperature)

## Preparation

1. Preheat oven to 350ºF (180°C).
2. Grease a 10 or 12-cup bundt cake pan (26cm) with soft shortening, covering every angle, nook and cranny. Dust with flour, shaking off excess. Reserve.
3. Sift 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour, 2 ½ teaspoons baking powder and ½ teaspoons salt. Set aside.
4. Beat ¾ cup unsalted butter until creamy in a large bowl. Slowly add 1 ¾ cup sugar and beat for 2 minutes.
5. Add 3 eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition, and then beat for 2 more minutes.
6. Add 2 teaspoons ground fresh cardamom, ½ teaspoon vanilla extract and 1 teaspoon lemon zest if using it.
7. Beginning and ending with dry ingredients, add them in 3 parts alternating with ¾ cup whole milk and ½ cup sour cream in 2 parts.
8. Pour batter into prepared pan, spreading evenly.
9. Bake for about 45-50 minutes or until a cake tester or toothpick comes out clean. Depending on the type of pan and oven you use, it might take more. So always check and take baking times given in a recipe as a reference.
10. Let cool 5-10 minutes and carefully invert onto a wire rack.
11. Cool completely. Cover in plastic wrap until ready to serve.
12. Dust with powdered sugar before serving. Or use a glaze (see Notes below).
