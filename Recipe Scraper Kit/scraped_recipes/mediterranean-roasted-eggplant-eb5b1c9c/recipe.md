# Mediterranean Roasted Eggplant

**Source:** https://succulentrecipes.com/mediterranean-roasted-eggplant/
**Author:** Emily
**Yield:** ['4', '4 servings']
**Prep time:** PT10M
**Cook time:** PT20M
**Total time:** PT30M

This Mediterranean Roasted Eggplant is a celebration of flavors and texture that truly showcases the beauty of this versatile vegetable. This dish is not just about the eggplant, though; it’s infused with Mediterranean spices and ingredients like garlic, olive oil, and fresh herbs, which add depth and brightness to every bite.

## Ingredients

- 2 medium eggplants, sliced into 1-inch rounds
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

## Preparation

### Preheat the Oven

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
### Prepare the Eggplant

2. Slice the eggplants into 1-inch rounds and place them in a large bowl.
### Add the Olive Oil and Spices

3. Drizzle the olive oil over the eggplant slices and sprinkle with garlic powder, smoked paprika, cumin, oregano, salt, and pepper.
### Toss to Coat

4. Toss the eggplant slices until they are evenly coated with the oil and spices.
### Arrange on Baking Sheet

5. Spread the seasoned eggplant slices in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.
### Roast in the Oven

6. Roast in the preheated oven for 20 minutes or until the eggplant is tender and golden brown.
### Garnish and Serve

7. Remove from the oven and garnish with freshly chopped parsley before serving.
