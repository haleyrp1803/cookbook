This almond milk latte is sweetened using homemade cardamom syrup. It’s smooth, creamy and enhanced by gentle hints of vanilla, cardamom and rosewater. If you enjoy flavoured lattes give this one a go.

## Ingredients

- 100 g Demerara sugar
- 100 ml Water
- ⅜ teaspoon Ground green cardamom
- ½ teaspoon Vanilla extract
- 5 drops Rosewater ((optional))
- 75 ml Espresso coffee
- 150 ml Almond milk ((barista blend))
- 30 ml Cardamom syrup

## Preparation

### Make the Cardamom Syrup

1. Put the sugar and ground cardamom in a small pan with the water and heat gently, stirring frequently until the sugar dissolves.
2. Take the pan off the heat and leave the syrup to infuse for 30 minutes.
3. Add the vanilla extract and (if using it) the rose water.
4. Strain through a nut bag or a piece of muslin and decant the cardamom syrup into a bottle or jar.
### Make the Almond Milk Latte

5. Brew the espresso coffee either using a coffee machine or using an Aeropress, a moka pot or a cafetiere (French press).
6. Pour a measure of cardamom syrup into a mug and add the espresso.
7. Steam or heat the milk and pour in, mixing with a spoon if desired (or try some latte art). Serve immediately.
