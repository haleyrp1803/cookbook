## Ingredients

- 3 medium-sized apples (Granny Smith or Honeycrisp)
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup milk

## Preparation

1. Prepare the Apples: Peel, core, and slice apples into thin wedges.
2. Make the Caramel Base: Melt butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon in a saucepan; pour into a greased cake pan.
3. Arrange the Apples: Layer apple slices over the caramel mixture.
4. Prepare the Cake Batter: Mix dry ingredients in one bowl; cream sugar and eggs in another, then combine.
5. Combine and Bake: Pour batter over apples and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 40 minutes.
6. Cool and Serve: Let cool for 10 minutes, then invert onto a serving plate.
