An easy, healthy blackberry mocktail with lime and mint

## Ingredients

- 5 Blackberries
- 1 tbsp Lime juice
- 4 Mint leaves
- 1 tsp Honey
- 1 cup Sparkling water (or your choice of kombucha)

## Preparation

1. Muddle blueberries, lime and honey in a glass until juice is released from the blackberries.
2. Clap the mint together in your hands to release the scent, then add to the glass.
3. Top with sparkling water or kombucha.
4. Garnish with a lime wheel and serve.
