This amazing brown sugar BBQ chicken recipe is the perfect BBQ main. The best part? The slow cooker does all the work for you!

## Ingredients

- 2 lb. boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1 c. barbecue sauce, plus more for serving
- 1/2 c. packed light or brown sugar
- 1/4 c. bourbon
- 1/4 c. Italian dressing
- 2 tsp. garlic powder
- 1 tsp. paprika
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 6 potato buns
- Coleslaw, for serving

## Preparation

1. In a slow-cooker, combine chicken breasts, barbecue sauce, brown sugar, bourbon, Italian dressing, garlic powder and paprika. Season with salt and pepper.
2. Toss until well coated, then cover and cook on high for 4 hours or on low for 6 hours.
3. Shred chicken and serve on buns with a drizzle of barbecue sauce and a spoonful of coleslaw.
