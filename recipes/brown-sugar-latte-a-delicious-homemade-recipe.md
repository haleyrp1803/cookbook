This Brown Sugar Latte is a simple recipe that combines espresso, milk, brown sugar, cinnamon and a bit of nutmeg to create a delicious treat

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Milk (- or non-dairy alternative)
- 2 shots Espresso (- or ½ cup of strongly brewed coffee)
- 2 tbsp Brown Sugar
- 1 tsp Vanilla Extract
- ¼ tsp Ground Cinnamon
- Whipped Cream (- optional)
- 1 dash Ground Nutmeg

## Preparation

1. Brew 2 shots of espresso, and add it into your chosen mug
2. Steam the milk with milk frother or small saucepan over medium heat. Heat until it’s hot but not boiling.
3. Add in the brown sugar, cinnamon and vanilla extract. Stir until the brown sugar & cinnamon is fully dissolved into the milk.
4. Add the milk into the Chosen Mug with the espresso and stir together
5. Complete the latte by adding in the optional whipped cream and dash of ground nutmeg
6. Enjoy your Brown Sugar Latte
