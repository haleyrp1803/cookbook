This gorgeous blood orange mocktail recipe is so simple to make and just divine to drink. Perfect for Christmas or other special occasions.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons blood orange juice (freshly squeezed)
- 1 tablespoon lime juice (freshly squeezed)
- 1 cup alcohol free Rosé or alternative (see notes)

## Preparation

1. Fill a large wine glass with ice. Take a slice of blood orange and press it against the inside of the glass.
2. Add the blood orange juice, lime juice and alcohol free rosé (or alternative). Stir gently.
3. Garnish with a sprig of rosemary, thyme or mint.
4. Serve immediately.
