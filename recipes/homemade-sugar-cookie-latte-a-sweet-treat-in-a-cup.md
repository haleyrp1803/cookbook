This sugar cookie latte is a delicious recipe to give you Christmas vibes in a cup, no matter what time of year it is. Just try it!

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Milk (- or non-dairy alternative)
- 2 shots Espresso
- 2 tbsp Granulated Sugar
- 1 tbsp Vanilla Syrup (- our Natural Vanilla Syrup)
- ½ tsp Almond Extract
- ¼ tsp Ground Cinnamon
- Whipped Cream (- optional)
- 1 dash Nutmeg

## Preparation

1. Brew a double shot of espresso (or a strong 1/2 cup of coffee) and pour into your chosen mug
2. Steam milk with milk frother or small saucepan over medium heat. Heat until it’s hot but not boiling.
3. Add the other ingredients including the sugar, vanilla syrup, almond extract, and ground cinnamon to the hot milk. Stir until the ingredients are all completely dissolved into the milk.
4. Add the milk mixture to the espresso or coffee
5. Top it off with whipped cream (if desired) and add in the dash of nutmeg for holiday flavor.
6. Enjoy your Homemade Sugar Cookie Latte!
