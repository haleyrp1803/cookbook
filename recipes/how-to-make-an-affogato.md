an Italian dessert with espresso and ice cream

## Ingredients

- 1-2 shots espresso
- 1-2 scoops vanilla ice cream

## Preparation

1. Brew the espresso.
2. In a cup or small bowl, add a scoop or two of ice cream.
3. Pour the hot espresso over the ice cream and serve.
