This marry me gnocchi is our newest take on the Delish original viral recipe, ready in 30 minutes with minimal ingredients.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp. extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 3/4 c. finely chopped sun-dried tomatoes in oil
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp. fresh thyme
- 1/2 tsp. crushed red pepper flakes
- 1 (17-oz.) pkg. gnocchi
- 1 1/4 c. low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
- 1/2 c. heavy cream
- 1/2 c. shredded Parmesan
- 2 tbsp. fresh torn basil

## Preparation

1. In a large skillet over medium heat, heat 1 Tbsp. oil. Add tomatoes and cook, stirring occasionally, until just starting to brown, 2 to 3 minutes. Add garlic, thyme, and red pepper flakes and cook, stirring, until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes more.
2. Push tomato mixture to edges of skillet and pour remaining 1 Tbsp. oil into the center. Add gnocchi and toss to coat in oil. Cook, undisturbed, 2 minutes, then stir in broth and cream to combine with tomato mixture. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi is tender and sauce is slightly reduced, 4 to 5 minutes.
3. Stir in Parmesan. Top with basil.
