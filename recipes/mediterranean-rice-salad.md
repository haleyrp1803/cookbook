This delicious and vibrant Mediterranean Rice Salad is so easy to make and the perfect addition to a BBQ or summer dinner party.

## Ingredients

- 200 g Brown rice
- 2 Bell peppers (diced)
- 0.5 Cucumber (diced)
- 200 g Cherry tomatoes (quartered)
- 100 g Sweetcorn
- 1 Red onion (diced)
- 235 g Butter beans (canned; drained weight)
- 50 g Spinach (chopped)
- 50 g Sun-dried tomatoes (drained and chopped)
- 100 g Mixed olives (chopped)
- 40 g Feta (crumbled)
- 30 g Pine nuts
- 6 g Fresh dill (finely chopped)
- 3 tbsp Olive oil
- 2 tbsp Apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp Honey
- 2 Garlic clove (crushed)
- 2 pinch Sea salt and ground black pepper
- 1 Lemon (juice only)

## Preparation

1. Cook 200 g of Brown rice according to the packet instructions.
2. Add the cooked rice to a large serving dish.
3. Add 2 Bell peppers, 0.5 Cucumber, 200 g of Cherry tomatoes, 100 g of Sweetcorn, 1 Red onion, 235 g of Butter beans, and 50 g of Spinach.
4. Add 50 g of Sun-dried tomatoes, 100 g of Mixed olives, 40 g of Feta, 30 g of Pine nuts and 6 g of Fresh dill.
5. Finish with 3 tbsp of Olive oil, 2 tbsp of Apple cider vinegar, 1 tbsp of Honey, 2 Garlic clove, 2 pinch of Sea salt and ground black pepper and the juice of 1 Lemon.
6. Mix well to combine everything.
