This Mediterranean Roasted Eggplant is a celebration of flavors and texture that truly showcases the beauty of this versatile vegetable. This dish is not just about the eggplant, though; it’s infused with Mediterranean spices and ingredients like garlic, olive oil, and fresh herbs, which add depth and brightness to every bite.

## Ingredients

- 2 medium eggplants, sliced into 1-inch rounds
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

## Preparation

### Preheat the Oven

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

### Prepare the Eggplant

1. Slice the eggplants into 1-inch rounds and place them in a large bowl.

### Add the Olive Oil and Spices

1. Drizzle the olive oil over the eggplant slices and sprinkle with garlic powder, smoked paprika, cumin, oregano, salt, and pepper.

### Toss to Coat

1. Toss the eggplant slices until they are evenly coated with the oil and spices.

### Arrange on Baking Sheet

1. Spread the seasoned eggplant slices in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.

### Roast in the Oven

1. Roast in the preheated oven for 20 minutes or until the eggplant is tender and golden brown.

### Garnish and Serve

1. Remove from the oven and garnish with freshly chopped parsley before serving.
