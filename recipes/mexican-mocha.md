Mexican mocha is a playful and warming take on a classic mocha drink. This Latin-inspired spicy mocha is an exciting combination of coffee, chocolate and spices creating a deeply comforting drink with a kick of chilli heat.

## Ingredients

- 150 ml Strong coffee (freshly brewed using 18g ground coffee per 75ml)
- 300 ml Milk (full fat)
- 30 g Dark chocolate (with 70% cocoa solids)
- 1 tablespoon Honey
- ½ teaspoon Ground cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon Ground nutmeg
- ¼ teaspoon Ground allspice
- ¾ teaspoon Ancho chilli powder (or other chilli powder of your choice)
- Pinch of salt
- Whipped cream
- Grated chocolate
- Ancho chilli powder
- Ground cinnamon or a cinnamon stick

## Preparation

1. Warm the milk (but don't let it boil). Take it off the heat and stir in the honey, cinnamon, nutmeg, allspice and the chilli powder. Leave it to infuse for 15 minutes.
2. Strain through a piece of muslin or a nutbag and return the infused milk to a clean pan.
3. Chop the chocolate and add it to the pan with a pinch of salt. Heat to melt the chocolate, stirring constantly. Take off the heat when the temperature reaches 70C/ 158F.
4. Brew the coffee and pour it into the pan. Stir.
5. Optional: froth the spicy mocha drink with the steam wand on your coffee machine, a dedicated milk foamer or a stick blender.
6. Pour it into warmed mugs or coffee glasses.
7. Top with cream (if desired) and garnishes then serve immediately.
