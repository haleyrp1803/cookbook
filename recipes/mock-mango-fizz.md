Fruity and refreshing, this mango mocktail has all the trappings of a cocktail -- except the tequila. (Add a splash if you miss it; we won't tell.)

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup orange juice
- 2 tablespoons grapefruit juice
- 2/3 cup mango-flavor seltzer
- Orange twist, for garnish

## Preparation

1. Add the orange juice and grapefruit juice to a 10- to 14-ounce Tom Collins glass. Add crushed ice to fill the glass. Top off with the seltzer. Garnish with the orange twist.
