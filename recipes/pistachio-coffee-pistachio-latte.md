Pistachio coffee is an absolute treat to sip upon with sweet, creamy & nutty elements to savour. This pistachio latte recipe is quick & easy to make. It's also far cheaper than buying one from a coffee shop.

## Ingredients

- 80 ml Espresso coffee (made from 18g espresso powder)
- 150 ml Milk (Whole, Semi-skimmed, Dairy-free)
- 30 g Pistachio cream
- ¼ teaspoon Vanilla extract
- ⅛ teaspoon Sea salt
- Whipped cream
- Pistachio cream
- Chopped pistachio nuts

## Preparation

1. Fill the serving cup/mug/glass with hot water and set aside.
2. Warm the milk in a milk pan to 70℃/ 160℉.
3. Stir in the salt, vanilla and pistachio cream using a small handheld whisk.
4. Brew the espresso coffee, add to the pan and reheat to 70℃/ 160℉ whisking continuously.
5. Pour the hot water out of the serving mug and pour your coffee into it.
6. Add toppings as desired and serve immediately.
