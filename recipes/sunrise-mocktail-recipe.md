This easy sunrise mocktail is perfect for 1 or a crowd.

## Ingredients

- Alcohol-free sparkling wine (or alternatives (see note 1))
- Orange juice
- 1/2 teapsoon Grenadine

## Preparation

1. Add equal amounts orange juice and non-alcoholic sparkling wine (or alternative) to a champagne flute.
2. Gently pour in the grenadine. Refer to the video below where I demonstrate how to do this.
3. Garnish with your choice of fruit and serve.
