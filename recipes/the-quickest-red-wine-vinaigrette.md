This easy recipe for tangy red wine vinegar dressing has a kiss of Dijon mustard and maple syrup for balance. Highly adaptable, it comes together in a flash.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- ¼ cup red wine vinegar
- 1 Tbsp. Dijon mustard
- 1 Tbsp. pure maple syrup
- 1¼ tsp. Diamond Crystal or ¾ tsp. Morton kosher salt
- ½ tsp. freshly ground black pepper
- ¼ tsp. garlic powder

## Preparation

1. Combine ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil, ¼ cup red wine vinegar, 1 Tbsp. Dijon mustard, 1 Tbsp. pure maple syrup, 1¼ tsp. Diamond Crystal or ¾ tsp. Morton kosher salt, ½ tsp. freshly ground black pepper, and ¼ tsp. garlic powder in a small glass jar or other container with a tight-fitting lid. Screw on lid or cover tightly and shake until emulsified. Do ahead: Vinaigrette can be made 1 month ahead. Keep covered and chill.
