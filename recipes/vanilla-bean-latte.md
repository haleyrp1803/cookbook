Vanilla bean latte is easy to make at home and it tastes so much better than coffeeshop versions. This quick and simple vanilla bean coffee can be enjoyed hot or iced.

## Ingredients

- 75 ml Espresso coffee (freshly brewed using 18g espresso powder)
- 20 ml vanilla bean syrup
- 150 ml Whole milk (or dairy-free alternative of your choice)

## Preparation

1. Warm your coffee mug or glass by filling it with hot water.
2. Brew the espresso coffee.
3. Steam the milk until warm, creamy, and frothy. You can use a milk frother, steam wand, or gently heat the milk on the stove and whisk vigorously. Note: Do not heat it above 70℃/ 140℉ as it can become bitter.
4. Discard the hot water from the mug/ glass. Pour in the syrup and the espresso coffee.
5. Pour the steamed milk onto the coffee, stir and serve immediately (or top with whipped cream ahead of serving).
