Start your mornings or enjoy your afternoons with this easy Vanilla Chai Latte Recipe. You'll save so much money making your own!

## Ingredients

- 8 oz boiling water
- 2 chai tea bags
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 8 oz whole milk

## Preparation

1. Combine the water and tea bags. Let steep for 5 minutes. Add the sugar and stir until it dissolves. Add the vanilla.
2. Meanwhile, bring the milk to a gentle simmer. Alternatively, you can heat the milk in the microwave.
3. Combine the tea mixture with the hot milk in a large mug.
