This classic watermelon agua fresca recipe is easy to make and perfect to sip on warm days.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups cubed watermelon
- 2 cups water
- 1/4 cup lime juice
- Your choice of mint, lime or watermelon wedges for garnish
- 1-3 tablespoons honey
- 1-3 tablespoons powdered monkfruit sweetener
- 1-3 tablespoons sugar
- A few drops liquid Stevia

## Preparation

1. Place watermelon, sweetener and 1 cup of water into a high speed blender. Blend well.
2. Using a teaspoon, test the sweetness. Add more if you prefer, then blend again.
3. Strain liquid into your pitcher and add the remaining 1 cup of water.
4. Add ice to your glasses and pour the agua fresca over the top.
5. Add some fresh lime juice to each glass and stir. Taste and add more lime if needed.
6. Garnish with your choice of lime wheels, mint or watermelon wedges.
