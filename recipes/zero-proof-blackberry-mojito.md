The unique combination of fresh thyme and blackberries give this alcohol-free riff on a mojito just as much sophistication and interest as it’s boozier counterparts.

## Ingredients

- 5 blackberries
- 1 to 2 sprigs thyme, plus more for garnish
- 2 teaspoons superfine sugar
- Ice
- 1 ounce lemon juice
- Sparkling cider
- Lemon wheel, for garnish

## Preparation

1. Muddle the blackberries, thyme sprigs and sugar in a glass. Fill with ice and the lemon juice; top with sparkling cider. Garnish with a lemon wheel and thyme sprig.
